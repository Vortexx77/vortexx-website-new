## 🎯 Design Principles Applied

### 1. **Visual Hierarchy Preserved**
- Headings remain **bold and impactful**
- Clear distinction between h1, h2, h3
- Font weights maintained (black for headings)

### 2. **Breathing Room**
- Reduced padding creates tighter, cleaner layouts
- Whitespace remains intentional
- Components feel more cohesive

### 3. **Text Readability**
- Body text reduced from 16-24px to 12-18px
- Line heights maintained for readability
- Labels and badges more subtle at 10px

### 4. **Component Proportions**
- Cards and sections scaled proportionally
- Border radius reduced for sharper, cleaner look
- Icons sized appropriately for context

### 5. **Elegant Spacing**
- Section padding reduced by 30-50%
- Gap between elements tightened
- Overall vertical rhythm improved
